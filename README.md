Freelance BI/data consultancy (Nordic Raven Solutions) helping Danish SMEs enrich their
internal data (Business Central, e-conomic, etc.) with free external registries (BBR,
CVR, DMI weather, and more as needed).

Pre-revenue, zero clients as of 2026-08-17. Everything here is deliberately scoped
narrow until there's a real client to validate direction against.
